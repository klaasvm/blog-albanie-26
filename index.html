The route

Day by day

  1. 1Day 1

    Arrive

    After a 2.5-hour flight from Belgium, we landed in Tirana. We walked to our accommodation and got a good night's rest.

  2. 2Day 2

    Long bus ride

    We took an 8-hour bus ride from Tirana to Sarandë. In the evening, we were warmly welcomed by the owners of our stay. That evening, we played a lot of fun drinking games! Later that night, we walked to the coast of Sarandë to watch the football match: Belgium vs. Senegal. After winning the game following a stressful comeback, we went for a swim in the sea.

  3. 3Day 3

    Blue eye

    We took a short bus ride to Finiq to see the Blue Eye. It was beautiful.

  4. 4Day 4

    Boat trip

    A boat was waiting for us at the coast in the morning, and we took a 6-hour ride on it with multiple swim stops. This was a lot of fun!

  5. 5Day 5

    Nightclub

    On day 4, we took a rest day. We chilled at our accommodation and played some games. In the evening, we went to a club. This was super cool, although the entrance and the vibe of the club itself were a bit strange.

  6. 6Day 6

    Sunset

    After that late night, we took another rest day. In the evening, we watched the sunset from the top of a nearby mountain. The hike up the mountain was amazing with lots of fun moments.

  7. 7Day 7

    Arrive in villa

    Today was another long bus ride back to the north of Albania. When we arrived, we instantly went for a swim in our new stay: a big villa.

  8. 8Day 8

    Villa

    We chilled a lot at the villa, played many games, and I even went for a walk to the beach, which was amazing. Something very cool about this area was that there were many open, abandoned buildings where you could sit and have a beautiful view.

  9. 9Day 8

    Leave

    After some of the best days of my life, we called a taxi back to the airport. After eating a huge bucket of chicken nuggets, we caught our flight back to Belgium.
